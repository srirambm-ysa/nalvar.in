Chapter 01 — Munaiyaduvar Nayanar

Thiruchitrambalam. Periyapuranam, Part 985, authored by the divine Sekkizhar Peruman. Karaikkandan Sarukkam, Munaiyaduvar Nayanar Puranam. Following the Vayilar Nayanar Puranam in the divine Periyapuranam authored by Sekkizhar Peruman, comes the Munaiyaduvar Nayanar Puranam. Firstly, in the Thokai Nool, Thiruthondath Thokai, composed by our Lord Sundaramoorthy Swamigal, in the eighth verse, this Nayanar is praised as "I am a devotee of Araikonda Vel Nambi Munaiyaduvar." Munaiyaduvar Nayanar was a person of great valor. If those who were defeated by their enemies in battle came to him and asked for his help, if there was justice on the side of the defeated, this Nayanar would lead an army in support of the defeated, achieve victory, and help them. In return, they would give him great wealth for having helped them achieve victory. He would then use this wealth to perform services for Lord Shiva and Shiva's devotees. Therefore, Sundaramoorthy Swamigal praises him as "I am a devotee of Araikonda Vel Nambi Munaiyaduvar." "Araikonda Vel Nambi" means Munaiyaduvar, a great man who wielded weapons capable of cutting down and defeating enemies. Our Lord Sundaramoorthy Swamigal praises him, saying, "I am a devotee of such a Munaiyaduvar Nayanar." Now, in the Vagai Nool, Thiruthondar Thiruvandathi, in the 62nd verse, Nambiandar Nambigal Peruman praises this Nayanar. That verse is as follows: "Nidur Adhipan Munaiyaduvan endrum amarul azhindavarkka kooli yetru erindhu venra perum selvam ellam kanaga nan meru ennum kundram valaitha silaiyan thamarkku koduthanane." The meaning of this verse is that elders say that Munaiyaduvar Nayanar, the leader who was born in the sacred place called Thirunidur, would lead an army for those defeated in battle, receive payment for it, and then give all the great wealth thus earned to the devotees of Lord Shiva, who bent the golden Meru mountain as a bow. Now, in the Viri Nool, the Periyapuranam authored by the divine Sekkizhar Peruman, the Munaiyaduvar Nayanar Puranam is the 52nd Puranam. The divine Sekkizhar Peruman has sung the Munaiyaduvar Nayanar Puranam in six verses. In the ninth Sarukkam, Karaikkandan Sarukkam, the Munaiyaduvar Nayanar Puranam is the fifth Puranam. Now, the first verse: "Maaru kadindhu man kaatha vallavar ponni thirunattu naaru viraippa pooncholaiyil nanai vai thirandhu pozhi sezhunththen aaru perugi vellamidum mallal vayalin mallar uzhum seru naru vasam kamazhum selva nidur thirunidur." In the sacred land of the Cholas, where the Kaveri flows, there is a sacred place called Thirunidur. It is Thirunidur, where wealth abounds. The meaning of this verse is that even the mud in the fields, where farmers plow, smells fragrant because of the rich honey flowing from the blooming flower buds in the fragrant groves, which overflows like a river. Even the mud, which should smell bad due to the decomposition of manure and other substances, instead smells good because the water mixed with honey from the groves flows into it. The divine Sekkizhar Peruman thus sings about the prosperity of Thirunidur, a town rich in such wealth, in praise of its urban splendor. He sings, "Selva Nidur Thirunidur." Just as with Thiruamoor, Thiruamoor, Kamazh Sarur, Kancharur, we see the divine poetic excellence of Sekkizhar Peruman here as well. This Thirunidur is the 21st sacred place on the northern bank of the Kaveri in the Chola country. It is a place praised as Vagularanyam, Magizhvanam. It is called Thirunidur because it remains undestroyed even during the dissolution of the universe. Indra, Surya, Chandra, and a Brahmin named Tanmasuthan, who was born due to a curse, and Kalai, known as Alala Sundari, all worshipped Lord Shiva here and attained blessings. Our Lord Sundaramoorthy Swamigal, while passing by this place without worshipping, by divine grace, returned here and sang a patikam, "Nidur Paniyavadalame," and worshipped, making it a glorious place. The Lord's form is a very red Jyotirlinga. The Lord's names are Arutsomanathar, Kaanatheertha Sankarar, and the Goddess is Veyuru Tholi Ammai, Adhikanthi Ammai. There are nine sacred tanks, including Chandra Pushkarani. The sacred tree is the Magizha tree. Thirunidur is a place that has received a patikam, "Thiruppunkur and Thirunidur," thus are the glories of Thirunidur. Now, the second verse: "Vilangum vanmai mikkulla velan thalaikkudi mudalvar kalankon midatrukan nudalar kazhalil serindha kadhal mikum ulankol thiruthondin urimaiyin ullar nallar munai arindha valam kodu iraivar adiyarkku marathu alikkum vaymaiyar." In Thirunidur, Munaiyaduvar Nayanar belongs to a prominent family of Vellalas, known for their generosity and strength. He has a deep and intense devotion to the feet of Lord Shiva, whose throat is dark. He is committed to performing sacred service with a sincere heart. He is truthful in giving the wealth earned from victories in battle to the devotees of the Lord without fail. The third verse: "Maatraarkku amaril azhindhor vandhu thambal manidhiyam aatrum parisu pesinal adhanai naduvu nilai vaithu kootrum odungum aalvinaiyal kooli yetri sendru erindhu potrum venri kondu isaindha ponnum kondu mannuvar." Those who were defeated in battle would come to this Nayanar and offer great wealth, seeking his help. At that time, the Nayanar would stand impartially and examine their request. If there was dharma and justice on the side of the defeated, he would agree to help them. He would also negotiate a fee for leading the army in battle. They would offer great wealth. Our Nayanar would then lead an army on behalf of those defeated who had justice on their side, defeat the enemies, and accumulate the great wealth of gold they offered like a mountain. He would then spend that wealth on temple services and services for devotees. The fourth and fifth verses: "Inna vagaiyal petra nidhiyellam eesan adiyargal sonna sonnapadi nirambik koduthu thuyaponagamum kanna naruney kari thayir pal kaniul urutha kalandhu alithu mannum anbin neripirazha vazhiththondu aatri vaiginar. Matru innilaiamai pannadunal vaiya nigazha seithu vazhi utra anbin sennariyal umaiyal kanavan thiruvarulal petra sivalogathu amarndhu piriyya urimai maruvinar. Mutra vuzhandha munaiyaduvar ennum namam munnudaiyar." In this manner, he gave all the wealth he received as the devotees of Shiva requested, and he offered pure sacred food, sugar, fragrant ghee, curries, curd, milk, and fruits, all mixed together. He performed service in a way that never deviated from the path of enduring love. In this state, he performed sacred service prominently in this world for a long time, and through the righteous path of hereditary love, by the grace of Lord Shiva, the husband of Uma, he attained Sivaplokam. He obtained the inseparable right to remain in Sivaplokam forever. He is the great person who was specially named Munaiyaduvar because he fought victorious battles. Therefore, the name Munaiyaduvar is a descriptive name. This is not the Nayanar's birth name. The divine Sekkizhar Peruman explains this to us. Now, the sixth verse and the concluding verse of this Puranam: "Yavar eninum ikalarindhe eesan adiyar thamakkum inbam meva alikkum munaiyaduvar viraippom. Kamalakkazhal kazhal vanangi devar peruman saiva neri vilanga sengol murai puriyum kaval poonda kazharsingar thondin nilaimai katturaippam." The divine Sekkizhar Peruman says that after worshipping the lotus-like feet of Munaiyaduvar Nayanar, who, regardless of who the opponent was, would win battles and joyfully give the earnings to the devotees of the Lord, we will now describe the nature of the sacred service of Kazharsinga Nayanar, who, by the grace of Lord Shiva, rules with a righteous scepter to make the Shaiva path flourish in the world. The divine Sekkizhar Peruman concludes the Munaiyaduvar Nayanar Puranam, which he has been narrating until now, and states that he will now begin to narrate the Puranam of Kazharsinga Nayanar. The Kazharsinga Nayanar Puranam comes in the next Sarukkam. The Puranam of the five Nayanars in this Sarukkam, Karaikkandan Sarukkam, concludes with the Munaiyaduvar Nayanar Puranam. Praise, praise to the sacred feet of our Lord Munaiyaduvar Nayanar. Now, in the Thiruthondath Thokai, Sundaramoorthy Swamigal has sung, "Aruran Aruril Ammanukku Aale." This is how Sundaramoorthy Swamigal sang at the end of each verse of the Thiruthondath Thokai. Since it is a Thokai Nool, Nambiandar Nambigal Peruman, in the Vagai Nool, composed a verse suitable for this context as a praise to Sundaramoorthy Nayanar. Similarly, we see that at the end of each Sarukkam, the divine Sekkizhar Peruman includes a praise to Sundaramoorthy Swamigal. In that way, the concluding line for this Sarukkam is the line uttered by Sundaramoorthy Swamigal: "Aruran Aruril Ammanukku Aale." Its meaning is that Sundaramoorthy Swamigal sings this, saying, "I, Aruran, who am a servant to all the Nayanars mentioned in this Sarukkam, namely Kanampulla Nayanar, Kari Nayanar, Nindraseer Nedumara Nayanar, Vayilar Nayanar, and Munaiyaduvar Nayanar, and to all the Nayanars sung about so far, am also a servant to Amman, who resides in Thiruvarur." In accordance with this, Nambiandar Nambigal, in the 63rd verse of the Thiruthondar Thiruvandathi, a Vagai Nool, composes a verse as a praise to Sundaramoorthy Swamigal. It is as follows: "Koduthan mudalaikkol pillaiyirkku uyir andru pukkozhiyur thoduthan madhurakkavi avinasiyai vedar sutrum paduthan thirumurugan poondiyinil paraparathen maduthan avan enbar vanthondan agindra madhavanai." The meaning of this verse is that scholars say that Sundaramoorthy Swamigal, the Madhava known as Vanthondan, once composed sweet poems for Lord Avinasi, who resides in Pukkozhiyur. As a result, he brought back to life the child who had been swallowed by a crocodile. In Thirumurugan Poondi, he was surrounded and robbed by Shiva's ganas disguised as hunters. He is the one who drank the honey of the Supreme Being. The historical event of bringing back the child swallowed by a crocodile in Avinasi will be seen in great detail in the concluding Sarukkam, Vellana Sarukkam. Now, in the Viri Nool, the divine Sekkizhar Peruman's praise to Sundaramoorthy Nayanar, placed at the end of this Sarukkam, Karaikkandan Sarukkam, is the 4095th verse in the Periyapuranam. It is as follows: "Seri undu endru thiruthondil sinthai sellum payanukkum kuri undu ondragilum kurai ondru illom niraiyum karunaiyinal veriyun cholai thirumurugan poondi vedar vazhipparikkap pariyundavar em pazhavinai ver parippar ennum patrale." The divine Sekkizhar Peruman states that by the abundant grace of the Lord, Sundaramoorthy Swamigal, known as Nambi Arurar, worshipped the Lord of Thirumurugan Poondi when Shiva's ganas, disguised as hunters, robbed him of all his wealth on the path through Thirumurugan Poondi, surrounded by fragrant groves. There is a specific goal to be achieved through the sacred service of Sundaramoorthy Swamigal's feet, which is the attachment that comes from the thought that Nambi Arurar Peruman will uproot our old karma. Without that, we have no shortcomings. The divine Sekkizhar Peruman states that worshipping the sacred feet of our Lord Sundaramoorthy Swamigal alone will put an end to our birth and lead us to the liberation of moksha. He says that he will uproot the root of old karma. Old karma refers to the root karma. The root is the origin, and the cause of it is the vāsanā mala. Sundaramoorthy Swamigal will uproot all of these. Therefore, our cycle of birth will cease, and moksha will be attained. All of this happens by holding onto the sacred feet of Sundaramoorthy Swamigal, our Lord. Thus, the divine Sekkizhar Peruman states. The Karaikkandan Sarukkam thus concludes. Now, the tenth Sarukkam, Kadal Soozhndha Sarukkam, begins with the Kazharsinga Nayanar Puranam. We will continue to contemplate it. Praise, praise to the sacred feet of our Lord Sundaramoorthy Swamigal. Praise, praise to the sacred feet of Nambiandar Nambigal Peruman. Praise, praise to the sacred feet of the divine Sekkizhar Peruman. Praise, praise to the sacred feet of the commentator Sivakavimani Ayya. Thiruchitrambalam.
