மனம் உருகி பாடுபவர்கள் பரமனையே பாடுபவர்கள். பரமனையே பாடும் பொழுது இருக்கும் மனநிலை அவர்கள் சிவத்தோடு இரண்டறக் கலந்து நின்ற மெய் உணர்வோடு மனம் உருகி பாடுவார்கள். எனவே சிவனையே பொருளாக கொண்ட பாட்டுக்களையே பாடத்தக்கன ஆகும். மற்றவர்களை பொருள்களாக கொண்டவைகளெல்லாம் பாடத்தக்கன அல்ல என்பது பெறப்படுகின்றது. திருக்கோவையார் ஞான உலா முதலியவற்றின் பொருளும் கூட இதுவேதான். இவ்வாறு இசை தமிழை வைத்து பாடுபவர்கள் அதோடு மற்ற மொழிகளிலே அமைந்த கீதங்களை இறைவனாரை புகழும் கீதங்களை வைத்து பரமனையே பாடுபவர்கள். அவர்களது பண்பு இவ்வாறு அமைகின்றது. சிவபெருமானை உள்ளுரையாக கொண்ட இந்த கீதங்கள் தான் ஆன்மாக்களை உய்விக்கும். எனவே வடமொழி தென்மொழி இன்னும் மற்ற தேசத்து மொழிகள் எவைகளாக இருந்தாலும் சிவபெருமான் மகிமையையே முக்கிய பொருளாகக் கொண்டு படிக்கப் படிக்க தெவிட்டாத திவ்ய பாடல்கள் வடிவில் அமையும் நூல்களை கற்று கேட்டு சிந்தித்து உணர்ந்து தெளிந்து சிவத்தோடு இரண்டறக் கலந்து சிவனையே பாடுபவர்கள் பரமனையே பாடுபவர்கள். அத்தகைய பாடல்களை சிவபெருமான் பெரிதும் உவப்பார் என்பது பல்வேறு திருவாக்களிலே நாம் காண்கின்றோம். நாடும் நகரமும் நற்றிருக்கோயிலும் தேடித் திரிந்து சிவபெருமான் என்று பாடுமின் என்பது திருவாக்கு. ஆடும் பரிவேல் அணிசேவல் என பாடும் பணியே பணியாய் அருள்வாய் என்றும் பாட வேண்டும் நான் போற்றி என்றும் பாட்டான நல்ல தொடையாய் போற்றி என்றும் பத்திமையால் பணிந்து அடியேன் தன்னை பன்னாள் பாமாலை பாட பயில்வித்தானை என்றும் சிவபெருமான் இத்தகைய கீதப் பிரியராக இருக்கின்றார் என்பதனை இந்த திருவாக்களிலே நாம் காண்கின்றோம். காதபதிம் மேதபதிம் என்று ரிக்வேதம் சிவபெருமானை விதந்து ஓதுகின்றது. பாட்டுத் தலைவனும் யாகத் தலைவனும் என்று சிவபெருமானே இருக்கின்றார் என்பது இதனுடைய பொருள். இந்த வகையில் சர்வ முக்கியத்துவமும் மேன்மையும்

பண்பினையும் வாய்ந்த அருள் பாடல் பணியே தமது லட்சியப் பணியாகக் கொண்டு பிறரையும் பிறவற்றையும் பாடும் அந்தப் பண்புகளை எல்லாம் அறவே விட்டொழித்து மெய் உணர்வு மகிமையால் சிவபெருமானோடு இரண்டறக் கலந்து ஒன்றித்து நின்று இயல்பாக எழும் உள் உருக்கத்துடன் சிவனையே பாடும் நியம தலை நின்றவர்கள் தான் பரமனையே பாடுவார் ஆன தொகையடியார்கள். சிவனையே பொருளாகக் கொண்டு அது தென்மொழியாக இருந்தாலும் வடமொழியாக இருந்தாலும் மற்ற மொழிகளாக இருந்தாலும் இதில் ஏதேனும் ஒன்றில் சிவனையே பொருளாகக் கொண்ட இயல் இசைப் பாட்டுக்களை உரிய இசை வாய்த்த வகையால் ஒன்றிய மெய் உணர்வுடன் உள்ளுருகிப் பாடும் மெய்யடியார்கள் அவர்களே பரமனையே பாடுவார்கள் என்று போற்றப்படுகின்ற தொகையடியார்கள். என்று பரமனையே பாடுவார் புராணம் இவ்வாறு அமைகின்றது. இனி தொடர்ந்து சித்தத்தை சிவன் பாலே வைத்தார்கள் என்ற தொகையடியார்களின புராணத்தை தொடர்ந்து சிந்திப்போம். பரமனையே பாடுவார் திருவடிகள் போற்றி போற்றி. எம்பிரான் சுந்தரமூர்த்தி சுவாமிகள் திருவடிகள் போற்றி போற்றி. நம்பியாண்டார் நம்பிகள் பெருமான் திருவடிகள் போற்றி போற்றி. தெய்வ சேக்கிழார் பெருமான் திருவடிகள் போற்றி போற்றி. உரையாசிரியர் ஐயா திருவடிகள் போற்றி போற்றி. திருச்சிற்றம்பலம்.

# English

Chapter 01 — Paramanaiye Paduvar

Thiruchitrambalam. Periyapuranam, Part 995, composed by the divine Sekkizhar Peruman. The Puranam of Paramanaiye Paduvar, from the chapter "Pattharai Paniwar Sarukkam". In the 11th chapter of Periyapuranam, composed by the divine Sekkizhar Peruman, following the Puranam of Pattharai Paniwar, is the Puranam of Paramanaiye Paduvar. According to the sequence of Periyapuranam, this is the 59th Puranam, and the second Puranam in this chapter. In the compilation work called Thiruthondathogai, our Lord Sundaramurthy Swamigal sang about these collective devotees, "Paramanaiye Paduvar Adiyarkkum Adiyen" (I am a servant to the devotees who sing only of the Supreme Lord). These collective devotees are called "Paramanaiye Paduvar" (those who sing only of the Supreme Lord). Paraman is Lord Shiva. The collective devotees known as Paramanaiye Paduvar were characterized by their devotion to singing only of Lord Shiva. Earlier, we contemplated the characteristics of the collective devotees known as "Poiyadimai Illatha Pulavargal" (poets without false servitude). What was mentioned there were songs of pure Tamil (Iyal Tamil). Here, "Paramanaiye Paduvar" refers to songs that blend Iyal (literary form) with Isai (music). These collective devotees, Paramanaiye Paduvar, sing of the Supreme Lord through music and melodies. They were praised as those who sing songs of rich words and melodies, and as devotees who sing melodies extensively. The meaning of "I am a servant to the devotees who possess such a quality, Paramanaiye Paduvar" is this. Now, in the compilation work Thiruthondar Thiruvandhadhi, Nambiandar Nambigal Peruman has praised the collective devotees of Paramanaiye Paduvar in the 71st verse. That verse is as follows: "Thoguttha Vadamozhi Thenmozhi Yadondru Thondriyadhe Miguntha Iyal Isai Avvagaiyal Avvin Thoyum Netri Vaguttha Mathil Thillai Ambalaththan Malar Paathangal Mel Uguttha Manaththodum Paadavallor Enbar Utthamare." This is the verse. Specifically compiled languages are Sanskrit, the Northern language, and Tamil, the Southern language. Whichever of these two they mastered, they are considered excellent (Utthamare) if they are able to sing with a mind dedicated to the lotus-like feet of the Lord of Thillai Thiruchitrambalam, whose forehead touches the sky, through both the superior literary form (Iyal) and music (Isai). They are the collective devotees known as Paramanaiye Paduvar. Thus, the compilation work is structured. Now, in the elaborate work Periyapuranam, the Puranam of Paramanaiye Paduvar has been composed by the divine Sekkizhar Peruman in two verses. The Puranam of Paramanaiye Paduvar is the section that describes the characteristics of Paramanaiye Paduvar. The first verse is: "Puram Moonrum Settranaai Poonagam Anindhanaai Uranil Varum Oru Porulai Ulakanaitthum Ananaai Karanangal Kanamal Kannarndhu Niraindhanaai Paramanaiye Paduvar Tham Perumai Paduvam." He is the one who destroyed the three cities of the Asuras. Lord Shiva is the one who wears serpents as ornaments (Poonaga). He is the unique being who manifests when knowledge matures. The divine Sekkizhar Peruman mentions this in the verse as "Uranil Varum Oru Porul" (the unique being who comes through wisdom). Here, "Uran" refers to knowledge. Specifically, it refers to Pathijnanam (knowledge of the Lord). "Uranil Varum" means one who is known through Pathijnanam. Knowledge that occurs in the soul is of three types: Pasajnanam (knowledge of worldly things), Pasu Jnanam (knowledge of the individual soul), and Pathijnanam (knowledge of the Lord). Pasajnanam is the knowledge that occurs in the soul through the body, senses, and internal organs. It is the knowledge that God imparts to the soul through these worldly aggregates. Pasu Jnanam is the knowledge where the soul realizes itself as a distinct entity through self-awareness. Next, Pathijnanam is also of two types: investigative knowledge and experiential knowledge. Investigative knowledge is when one realizes, through scriptures and the grace-filled teachings of a guru, that "I am not this body," and that standing inseparably with Shiva is one's true nature and form. At this point, attachment to the body ceases, and one's knowledge clings to Shiva. Even in this state, Shiva does not appear clearly. However, because one constantly meditates on that Shiva-principle for Shiva to appear clearly, that meditation is called Shivajnanam. Now, experiential knowledge is the knowledge where, through that meditation, Shiva appears clearly, and the soul merges inseparably with Shiva, shining as Shiva itself. The Lord, who is the Pathi-principle, can only be known and experienced through Pathijnanam. He cannot be reached through Pasu Jnanam or Pasajnanam. He is beyond words and mind. He is beyond Pasu Jnanam and Pasajnanam, and is within the reach of Pathijnanam. Therefore, He is the unique being who comes through wisdom, and He is all the worlds. He is pervasive throughout the world. He manifests as an all-pervading presence, both within and without, in both inert and conscious universes. He creates all worlds through His Maya Shakti. He cannot be seen by instruments and organs. Nevertheless, He is the one who makes them see from within those instruments and organs. The divine Sekkizhar Peruman states that we shall sing the greatness of such a Supreme Lord, Paramanaiye Paduvar. The second verse is: "Thenthamizhum Vadakalaiyum Desigamum Pesuvana Mandrinide Nadampuriyum Vallalaiye Porulaga Ondriya Mey Unarvodum Ul Urugi Paduvar Panriyudan Pul Kana Paramanaiye Paduvar." These are the ones who understand that the Lord of Thillai Ambalam, the generous one who performs the dance, is the highest goal spoken of in all languages, be it Southern Tamil, Northern Sanskrit, or other regional languages. Here, "Thenthamizhum Vadakalaiyum Desigamum" appears. Here, "Vadakalai" refers to Sanskrit. It is generally called "Aariyam." The sacred utterances of Thevaram state, "Aariyaththodu Senthamizh Payan" (the benefit of pure Tamil with Aariyam) and "Thamizh Sollum Vadasollum Thazh Nizhal Sera" (Tamil words and Northern words join in the shade). Thirumandhiram praises, "Aariyamum Thamizhum Udane Solli" (speaking Aariyam and Tamil together). The sacred utterances of Thevaram praise, "Vadamoziyum Thenthamizhum Marai Nanum Anavan Kan" (He is the four Vedas, Northern language, and Southern Tamil). "Desigam" refers to languages of other regions. These are all spoken of, and the meaning indicated by these words is the sacred feet of Sri Natesa Peruman. Those who sing with a melting heart and a unified true understanding, considering only Sri Natesa Peruman as the object, are Paramanaiye Paduvar. When they sing of Paramanaiye Paduvar, their state of mind is one where they sing with a melting heart and a true understanding, having merged inseparably with Shiva. Therefore, only songs that have Shiva as their object are worthy of being sung. It is understood that songs that have others as their objects are not worthy of being sung. The meaning of Thirukkovaiyar, Gnana Ula, etc., is also the same. Thus, those who sing using musical Tamil, and those who sing of the Lord using melodies composed in other languages, are Paramanaiye Paduvar. Their characteristic is thus. These melodies, which have Lord Shiva as their inner core, will save souls. Therefore, regardless of whether they are in Sanskrit, Southern Tamil, or other regional languages, those who learn, listen to, contemplate, understand, and become clear about texts that take the glory of Lord Shiva as their main subject, and which are in the form of divine songs that never satiate, and who then merge inseparably with Shiva and sing only of Shiva, are Paramanaiye Paduvar. We see in various sacred utterances that Lord Shiva greatly delights in such songs. "Nadum Nagaramum Natthirukkoilum Thedi Thirintha Shivaperuman Endru Padumin" (Search for and wander in countries, cities, and good temples, and sing "Lord Shiva") is a sacred utterance. "Adum Parivel Aniseval Ena Padum Paniye Paniyay Arulvay" (Grant me the task of singing "Adum Parivel Aniseval") and "Padavendum Nan Potri" (I must sing praises) and "Pattana Nalla Thodaiyay Potri" (Praise to the one with good garlands of songs) and "Patthimaiyal Panindhu Adiyen Thannai Pannal Pamalai Pada Payilvitthanai" (He taught me, a humble servant, to sing garlands of songs for many days with devotion) – in these sacred utterances, we see that Lord Shiva is such a lover of melodies. The Rig Veda praises Lord Shiva as "Kathapathim Medapathim" (Lord of songs and Lord of sacrifices). Its meaning is that Lord Shiva Himself is the leader of songs and the leader of sacrifices. In this way, those collective devotees known as Paramanaiye Paduvar are those who, having made the gracious task of singing, which possesses all importance and excellence, their life's mission, have completely abandoned the qualities of singing about others and other things, and who, with the greatness of true understanding, have merged inseparably with Lord Shiva, and stand united, singing only of Shiva with an inner melting that arises naturally, having established themselves in this નિયમ (vow). Whether it is in Southern language, Northern language, or other languages, those true devotees who sing with a melting heart and a unified true understanding, with appropriate musical accompaniment, songs of literary and musical form that have Shiva alone as their object in any of these languages, are the collective devotees praised as Paramanaiye Paduvar. Thus, the Puranam of Paramanaiye Paduvar is structured. Now, we shall continue to contemplate the Puranam of the collective devotees who kept their minds on Shiva. Praises to the sacred feet of Paramanaiye Paduvar! Praises to the sacred feet of our Lord Sundaramurthy Swamigal! Praises to the sacred feet of Nambiandar Nambigal Peruman! Praises to the sacred feet of the divine Sekkizhar Peruman! Praises to the sacred feet of the commentator, Ayya! Thiruchitrambalam.
