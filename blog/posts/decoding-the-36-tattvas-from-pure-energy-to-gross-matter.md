If Saiva Siddhanta has a periodic table, it is the **36 tattvas** — elemental realities, or better, *levels of densification* — by which Pati (Shiva) appears as world, soul, and stone without ceasing to be Pati. The list is neither symbol nor metaphor; in Agamic usage a *tattva* is a real, efficacious tier of being with its own deity, mantra, and body-location. To learn tattvas is to learn anatomy of cosmos the way a physician learns anatomy of body: so that you can diagnose where experience snags and apply the right procedure at the right layer. Relegate tattvas to chart and you miss their clinical purpose.

The count 36 is not arbitrary. Five are *suddha* (pure) — Shiva, Shakti, Sadasiva, Isvara, Suddhavidya — where Shiva's sovereignty remains explicit. Seven are *suddha-asuddha* (mixed) or *vidya* tattvas — kala, niyati, kala, raga, vidya, purusha — plus maya itself — where Shakti begins to veil. Twenty-four are *asuddha* (impure) — antahkarana (3), jnanendriyas (5), karmendriyas (5), tanmatras (5), bhutas (5), plus prakriti and its evolutes — where world feels solid and choices feel independent. The descent is not fall but deployment — Shiva tactically self-limiting to give souls a stage where karma can be transacted and anava can ripen. Each step down is a condescension; each step up on return is initiation.

This essay decodes the ladder three ways: the pure five where creation begins without division, the mixed and impure descent where limitation becomes experience, and the mapping of that ladder onto temple and sadhaka body that makes it walkable. For canonical tables, see [Shaivam.org's illustrated 36 tattvas chart](https://shaivam.org/36-tattvas) and [WisdomLib's tattva etymology across systems](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/tattva). Manuscript source: [Project Madurai's Unmai Vilakkam verses 14–38 (tattva prakarana)](https://www.projectmadurai.org/pm_etexts/utf8/pmuni0456.html).

### 1. Five Pure Tattvas (Suddha): Where Shiva Becomes World Without Changing
The first five — often called *Siva-tattvas* — answer the hardest question in theodicy: how can world come from perfection without imperfection entering perfection? Siddhanta's answer is *shuddha-maya* — pure matter that is not yet resistant, like clear crystal that can take color without being stained. From **Siva tattva** — Shiva as *cit* (awareness) at rest, *shanta* — there flashes **Shakti tattva** — *unmukha*, will-to-create, Shiva's iccha. Shakti here is not consort but Shiva's own *svatantrya* (freedom). The pair are not two but one with self-difference, like sun and radiance. When Shakti wills to make knowledge possible, **Sadasiva** appears: “I am this” (*aham idam*) — Shiva knows himself as world-substrate. **Isvara** then tilts to “This is I” (*idam aham*) — world is foregrounded, self backgrounded. **Suddhavidya** balances as “I and This equally” — unity-in-diversity, the last moment where Shiva's agency is still felt as pervasive before withdrawal begins. These five are indexed linguistically as *nada, bindu, sadasiva, isvara, vidya* faces of *Om*. To chant Om properly is to retrace them.

Philosophically, the pure five secure *non-maya creation*. Unlike Samkhya where world evolves from *prakriti* alone, or Advaita where world is *vivarta* (apparent), Siddhanta says pure world is Shiva's *abhasa* — shining forth — not his modification. This protects grace: if world at root were merely impure matter, then embodiment would be exile; if root is still Shiva, then embodiment is pedagogy and return is memory rather than escape. The practitioner is not told to flee tattvas but to ascend them by being reinstalled at each tier by diksha. Diksha literally re-places mantra-seeds into each tattva layer.

Ritually, the pure five are located as five *brahma-mantras* in the practitioner's body during *nyasa*: Siva–Ishana (crown), Shakti–Tatpurusha (mouth/east), Sadasiva–Aghora (heart/south), Isvara–Vamadeva (genitals/north), Suddhavidya–Sadyojata (feet/west). This is why *Kamika Agama* spends pages on placement. The body becomes Agamic cosmology before mind understands it. One who feels Ishana at crown — not conceptually but as mantra-conductivity — already knows why impersonal Brahman cannot account for temple verticality.

Comparison sharpens. Kashmiri Shaivism's 36 overlap but read the pure five as manifestations of *prakasha-vimarsha*; Vedanta maps five *koshas* onto different base. Siddhanta's distinction is soteriological: the pure five are retrievable now through *nirbija* contemplation because they never left; the soul under anava only forgot to dwell there. Practices aimed at the pure tattvas are thus called *akramana* — not building something new but occupying what already shines. That is why Tirumoolar calls them *siva bhumi* — Shiva's ground.

A common mistake is to collapse the pure five into Vedantic *Saguna Brahman*. Resist. Saguna Brahman is Brahman qualified by maya; Suddhavidya is Shiva knowing himself through maya he owns but is not qualified by. The difference is self-possession versus being-possessed. See extended comparison in [Archive.org's translation of Tattva Prakasha with commentary](https://archive.org/details/36TattvasSaivaSiddhantaTranslation).

- **5 Suddha:** Siva, Shakti, Sadasiva, Isvara, Suddhavidya — Shiva's own self-display in *shuddha maya*, still sovereign.
- **7 Vidya + Maya:** Kala (time), Niyati (restriction), Kala (arts), Raga (desire), Vidya (limited knowledge), Purusha (bound soul), Maya (material cause) — the mixed hinge where limitation enters.
- **24 Asuddha:** Prakriti → antahkarana (buddhi, ahamkara, manas) → jnanendriyas/karmendriyas/tanmatras/bhutas — where world becomes own world.
- **Method:** Descent is *adho-mukha* (downward-facing Shakti), ascent is *unmukha* (initiation-reversed) — same ladder walked opposite direction.

### 2. Seven Mixed and Twenty-Four Impure: The Descent into Embodied Experience
The mixed seven are the hinge where diagnosis matters. **Maya** itself is not evil; it is the mother-tattva, described as *acit* but *nitya* — insentient yet eternal, like a loom. From it, Shiva through his Shakti draws five *kancukas* (jackets) that tailor infinity to finitude: **Kala** limits omnipotence to doership, **Niyati** fixes that doing in space, **Kalaa** (note spelling) limits authorship to particular capacities, **Raga** binds capacities to desire for completion, **Vidya** limits omniscience to knowable fragments. The sixth, **Purusha**, is the soul now jacketed — still eternal but tasting itself as small actor. This is perhaps the most compassionate cosmology in India: suffering is not punishment but pedagogical tailoring — infinity wears a uniform to learn economy.

These kancukas are experienced daily as “I can't,” “not here,” “I wish,” “I know a little.” Each is addressable by a specific *mantra-kala* and *adhva* (course). *Mr gendragama* lists six *adhvas* — mantra, pada, varna, bhuvana, tattva, kala — that map tattva descent onto chantable units. When a guru gives you *Samanya Diksha*, he manipulates *varna adhva* (phonemic) to affect *tattva adhva* — speech reroutes being. This is not symbolic; in Agamic realism, phoneme and tattva are co-causal. That is why Siddhanta preserves precise pronunciation where modern yoga imports approximate.

From Purusha, the **24 asuddha** cascade quickly because once soul is localized, it needs instruments. *Prakriti* (tri-gunate matrix) evolves *buddhi* (discernment), *ahamkara* (self-sense), *manas* (sorting mind) — the *antahkarana* triad. Then five *jnanendriyas* (hearing, touching, seeing, tasting, smelling) outward-facing, five *karmendriyas* (speech, hands, feet, excretion, generation) action-facing, five *tanmatras* (sound-taste-form-touch-smell essences) and five *bhutas* (ether, air, fire, water, earth). The order is generative: tanmatras (potentials) before bhutas (actuals). Knowing this order lets you reverse it in practice — earth to water to fire etc. — which is precisely what *bhuta-shuddhi* purification does before puja.

Two clinical implications follow. First, *karma* accumulates not at random but at the *ahamkara–buddhi* interface — where self-sense claims discernment's product as “mine.” Siddhanta's introspective practice therefore watches that seam: when a thought “I decide” arises, you tag its tattva lineage — *buddhi product claimed by ahamkara* — and release claim. Second, embodiment is not to be transcended but *rebalanced*: the 24 are not impurities to be destroyed but instruments to be tuned. A liberated being still has 24, but they are now *suddha*-modulated — body remains, as Jivanmukti proves.

Language note: *asuddha* does not mean sinful; it means *mixed with mala*. Even asuddha tattvas are still Shiva's maya-body. A Siddhanta master will say “all tattvas are Shiva; impurity is my forgetting, not their filth.” That forgetting is *anava*'s film, removable without trashing creation.

To see kancuka theory debated with Nyaya and Samkhya, consult [WisdomLib's kancuka entry with Siddhiyar citations](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/kancuka). The precision there rivals modern phenomenology of embodiment.

> “As the sun, though one, appears broken in many pots of water, so Shiva, though one, appears as many tattvas. Break the pots, not the sun; purify the tattvas, not creation itself.” — Tirumandiram 2345, glossed by Sivajnana Munivar on Isvara tattva.

### 3. Tattvas as Temple and Body: Mapping Cosmology onto Sadhana
Now the architecture that makes tattvas walkable. The South Indian temple is a 36-tattva map standing on earth. **Garbhagriha** (womb-chamber) is Siva tattva — dark, still, point (*bindu*). Its *linga* base (Brahma) = earth-water, mid (Vishnu) = fire, top (Rudra) = air-ether. The *vimana* rising above recapitulates suddha five; the *ardha-mandapa* and *maha-mandapa* embody vidya tattvas (transition); the *antar-pradakshina* rings carry bhuta and indriya placements; the *gopura* is cosmic *maya* — threshold between form and beyond-form. Walking from street through gopura, through mantapas, into garbha is ascent in descent — return is encoded in entry. This is why circumambulation is not tourism; it is tattva procession.

The human body, taught as *deha-alaya* (body-temple), receives the same mapping in *Thirumandiram* Tantra 2. Crown (brahmarandhra) = Siva, forehead = Sakti, throat = Sadasiva/Isvara zone, heart = Suddhavidya/Vidya, navel to pelvis = maya-prakriti-bhuta descent. *Kundalini* as described in Tirumoolar is not alien import but Agamic *kundali Shakti* — the coil of *vidya* energy waiting at *mula* to be led upward tattva by tattva. Bhuta-shuddhi before daily puja is rehearsal of tattva reversal: you dissolve earth into water with mantra, water into fire, fire into air, air into ether, ether into manas, manas into buddhi, buddhi into ahamkara, ahamkara into Purusha, Purusha into vidya, vidya into Shiva — then re-emit them enabled. The body practices cosmology until cosmology practices the body.

Sadhana thus becomes tattva hygiene. If you are stuck in *raga* (endless wanting), work on its tattva — *vidya* kala — via mantra that expands limited knowledge: *Tiruvasagam* chanting rather than more information. If stuck in *niyati* (fatalism: “I am where I am, cannot move”), work on *kalaa* — capacity — via seva that proves new capacities existir. The teacher diagnoses tattva-wise, not symptom-wise. Depression is not generic low mood; it may be *tamas-dominant prakriti* weighing on *buddhi*, best addressed via *kriya* (structured worship) that injects sattva before analysis. This granularity is why graduates of tattva study are steadier counselors — they know where to intervene.

Initiation (*diksha*) is formal tattva surgery. In *nirbija diksha*, guru places mantra into each of the 36, cuts the *pasa* filament at each knot (*granthi*), and rethreads it to Arul. The neophyte may feel little, but Agamic realism insists ontological rewiring occurred, much as suturing occurs even if patient is anesthetized. Future practice irrigates the suture. This is why Siddhanta asks for sustained *nitya-karma* (daily rites) after diksha — not as devotion quota but as post-operative physiotherapy for tattva-body.

Close by testing the map pragmatically. For one week, log experiences by tattva: note moments dominated by *aural* tanmatra (seeking sound), by *ahamkara* (defensiveness), by *raga* (craving completeness through purchase). You will discover the 36 are not metaphysics but immediate phenomenology. As [Archive.org's comparative tattva documents](https://archive.org/details/TattvaCategories) and a living Siddhanta teacher will confirm, the chart becomes humane the moment it ceases to be a wall poster and becomes a daily language: “I am not anxious; my buddhi is tamed by rajasic manas — let me do bhuta-shuddhi before deciding.”
